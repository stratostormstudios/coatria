# Studio acceptance: what is ready and what remains to prove

Status: Studio v1.6 implementation, 2026-09-18. This is a capability and acceptance checklist, not a deployment record. The last verified live release recorded for this update is v1.5, source `da87026`. Its CI passed 533 tests with four explicit skips on PostgreSQL 17.11. New v1.6 source and focused local evidence do not establish that v1.6 is deployed.

The implemented scope is a bounded procedural VFX pilot with shared company state, agent work and explicit human approvals. It does not establish a fully unattended VFX business. At this update the operator reports the pilot CPU and GPUs stopped; retained storage and billing reconciliation are separate from compute state.

## Acceptance matrix

**Fixture** means real application/API/database or browser execution with synthetic users, submissions or provider responses. **Local real media** means Blender actually rendered files and private storage actually verified their bytes. Neither establishes a live customer production.

The procedural profile consumes no client assets and produces a fixed original scene: at most 24 frames, 64–1,024 pixels per axis, 8 million aggregate pixels, scene-linear EXR in an approved color space. Client models, filmed footage, NAS transfer, general Nuke/Houdini work, custom creative generation, movie encoding and large resumable delivery require additional reviewed integrations. The client portal currently transports verified private sequences, up to 20 MiB per file; it is not a bulk footage-transfer service or EXR review player.

## Shortest honest live validation

1. Select the explicit one-shot preset: frames 1001–1004, no handles, 384 × 384, 24 fps, Linear Rec.709 EXR, lighting only. Keep the brief limited to original procedural output. Review finite CPU, inference, renderer and run allowances before starting resources.
2. Connect the producer/coordinator, specialist and separate planning reviewer. Refresh installations after host enrollment/initial connection, then approve finite coordination and planning-review policies against those revisions.
3. Activate the bounded coordinator mission and use **Run next cycle now** for the first cycle. Confirm actual producer submission, reviewer read/decision receipts and accepted planning state. Humans complete ingest and business gates.
4. Approve one exact render. Check the cloud-generated files, exact frame/specification coverage and every stored hash. Promote the verified version once; observe one specialist continuation submit the existing artifact for review.
5. Have the independent administrator inspect the real media and accept version, production, QC and delivery-handoff tasks. Prepare the exact package.
6. Confirm a real external recipient's account ID through an existing trusted channel. Create the scoped invitation, share it only through an authorized channel, and observe that account's response. Keep access issuance, file receipt and acknowledgement distinct.
7. Verify the host/renderer/inference cutoff, retained receipts and absence of duplicate work. Reconcile actual costs separately. Stop at a missing human/client prerequisite rather than bypassing it.

The owner must supply or involve the independent human reviewer and genuine external recipient to finish this live acceptance path. A sole owner can validate planning, rendering and pending review, but cannot honestly complete independent final acceptance alone. Rejected planning and client change requests still require a reviewed new production pass; fully automatic rework, business operations, multi-company fleet recovery and million-user capacity are not established.
