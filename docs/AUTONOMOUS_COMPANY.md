# Coatria: operating a company with agents

The product direction is a company operating system: an owner defines the business and measurable outcomes; named AI colleagues retain their roles; Coatria organizes permissions, recurring work, contributions and review. The office makes activity visible, while the same operations remain available through the API.

## Implemented foundation

- **Plugins:** eight curated bridges for Codex, Claude Code, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Fireworks, Together and Runpod. Versioned manifests specify permitted providers and models. Installation creates a scoped company identity; it does not purchase inference, connect a provider account, deploy a worker, or import arbitrary Slack plugins.
- **Organization and project work:** reviewed AI-production/VFX staffing plans create real paused agents, shared role instructions and explicit grants. Human/agent role bindings drive task responsibility. Typed projects carry dependencies, commercial/production gates and exact evidence; the coordinator can dispatch ready specialist work within a finite policy and lifetime allowance. Applying a staffing plan or scheduling a paused mission starts no compute.
- **Character:** role title, responsibilities/persona and working style travel with every installed-agent request. The fixed adapter policy keeps task accuracy, permissions and explicit AI identity above roleplay. This preserves configuration, not a fabricated personal history or guaranteed personality consistency from a stochastic model.
- **Company autopilot:** administrators define missions, assign existing agents, choose a 15–1,440 minute cadence and authorize at most 100 total cycles. The interface defaults to a paused three-cycle mission. Templates cover operations, delivery and quality. These are editable responsibilities, not automatically recruited departments.
- **Autonomous dispatch:** an online worker checks its own approved missions at startup and at most once per minute when idle. Scheduling, cycle allocation and durable run creation commit together. There is no backlog of catch-up cycles, and a mission cannot overlap its previous pending cycle. Several missions for one agent execute sequentially through its existing queue.
- **Progress:** every cycle has actual queued/running/succeeded/failed/cancelled state, a persisted result and the existing committed-action receipts. Results appear in company conversations. Successful execution does not prove a business outcome or approve a contribution.
- **Planning review:** a separate authorized reviewer may accept exact estimate/breakdown submissions, with reference planning explicitly selectable. Its policy, identity, expiry and run allowance are independently reviewed. This is machine planning acceptance, not media QC or a business approval.
- **Generated production and delivery:** Higgsfield request approval and job tracking connect to separately approved archives, typed image/video/audio versions, independent human QC, immutable packages and authenticated external-client downloads/responses. Project-file folders and stored versions have a real gateway implementation; native drive indexing remains metadata-only. The actual archive host and selected Runpod storage/gateway still need live qualification.
- **Verified-output continuation:** a newly approved, default-off policy can resume the original specialist once against its exact verified source to claim/register/submit the existing work. It uses the shared run allowance and has no generation, transfer, delegation or approval authority. An archive finishing does not start a worker or inference by itself.
- **Control:** current sponsor, mission-author and agent authority are checked during execution. Permission/configuration changes fence stale workers. Mission changes cancel pending work; they cannot undo earlier effects. Failed, cancelled or expired-lease mission execution pauses for administrator review instead of starting another reasoning attempt automatically.

## A first company

1. Write one concrete business outcome and success measure. For example: “Prepare a reviewable launch plan and prototype for product X,” rather than “run a successful business.”
2. For production work, review **Build an AI team** and its actual roles, grants, model settings and optional separate planning reviewer. For a smaller general task, install one operations lead with the necessary read/task grants. Connect a reviewed local or hosted worker through the matching setup guide; installation does not provision a server. Hosted sessions can continue without the customer's desktop when their actual worker is running and authorized.
3. Create a short mission in Company autopilot, review its objective, set a small cycle count and activate it. Observe the first results and committed actions before expanding responsibility.
4. Create a typed production project, approve the business gates and configure finite coordination/review policies. For generated media, explicitly approve the provider request and archive, then inspect exact stored evidence. Humans retain independent media/task acceptance; only the designated external client account may acknowledge delivery.
5. Pause or adjust missions when the business objective changes. Resolve ambiguous effects before resuming failed work. Enforce provider spending limits independently of Coatria cycle limits.

## Required before broader autonomous business operation

The intended service is **Coatria-managed cloud execution**. The repository now has reviewed single-company CPU provisioning, retained state, a scoped server inference broker, cancellation and finite reservations. Earlier Runpod pilots are recorded above; no present running host or fleet is inferred from them. Operators must qualify the chosen host/provider, inspect live leases and committed results, and verify shutdown/recovery behavior for each approved deployment. [Managed harness hosting](MANAGED_HARNESS_HOSTING.md) describes the service boundary.

Reviewed staffing, role ownership, dependency-aware planning, specialist delegation and forward generated-client delivery are implemented. Remaining business capabilities include autonomous hiring/commerce, contracts/payments, outbound sales, general production deployment, native NAS/workstation and arbitrary VFX execution, multi-tenant host fleets and cross-company personal-experience portability. These are not unlocked by adding another agent or token budget.

The candidate now represents client feedback → reviewed creative revision plan → newly authorized production → fresh independent QC → a new exact client package. Same-project revision rounds preserve prior tasks, approved carried media and client receipts. Newly scheduled generated coordinator missions can draft or select a source-bound plan, then wait for human application; existing mission objectives are unchanged. The verified-output continuation still finishes only one existing source-bound task. Technical specification, duration, deliverable-set and commercial scope changes need a separate scope-change workflow. See [generated revisions](STUDIO_GENERATED_REVISIONS.md).

The next operational proof is one current generation-to-client pilot on the qualified archive/storage deployment, followed by recovery, provider-capacity and multi-company isolation exercises. Durable inference/tool/transfer records exist, but uncertain provider mutations and unclean host recovery can still require reconciliation. Session/cycle/token reservations are not a comprehensive provider-invoice or company-wide spending cap. Evaluate quality and character consistency from accepted outcomes and evidence rather than token volume.

Before claiming a global production service, establish service objectives and alerting, backup/restore drills, incident response, queue and provider load testing, tenant-isolation testing, and measured capacity beyond the existing 50-session office test. A small controlled pilot is the appropriate deployment scope for the new autonomy layer.
